Kinetly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Kinetly is a mobile app that blocks distracting apps at a time you choose, until you hold a stretch in front of your camera. This page explains what data Kinetly collects, what it never collects, and why.

The short version

Your camera feed is analyzed entirely on your phone. No video, image, or body-position data is ever recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Kinetly collects a small amount of anonymous usage data to understand how the app is used, and nothing more.

Camera and pose detection

When you use the camera screen, Kinetly runs an on-device pose-detection model to check whether you are holding the selected stretch. This processing happens fully on your phone. Kinetly does not upload, store, or transmit your camera feed, any image, or any body-position data at any point, to Kinetly or to anyone else.

App blocking (Screen Time)

Kinetly uses Apple's own Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) to block the apps you choose at the time you set. This runs entirely on your device through Apple's system frameworks. Kinetly does not see which apps you actually open, does not receive your screen time data, and does not send your app selection anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your routines, schedules, settings, session history, and free-trial count are stored locally on your phone. This data is not uploaded to a server and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Analytics

Kinetly uses PostHog to record a limited set of anonymous product events (for example, which screen was opened, or that a stretch session was completed). This helps us understand which parts of the app are useful.

  • Events are anonymous and are not linked to your name, email, or any account.
  • Onboarding answers are sent only as category values (e.g. an age range), never as free text.
  • Screen recording / session replay is explicitly disabled.
  • Data is processed on PostHog's EU servers.
  • No data is collected for advertising, and Kinetly does not use any advertising identifier (IDFA).

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store using Apple's In-App Purchase system, and managed with the help of RevenueCat. Kinetly never sees or stores your payment details. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription status information from Apple in order to confirm your subscription across your devices.

Notifications

Reminders and shield notifications are scheduled locally and delivered by iOS itself. They are not routed through any Kinetly server.

Third parties

  • PostHog – anonymous product analytics (EU-hosted)
  • RevenueCat – subscription management
  • Apple – App Store purchases, Screen Time frameworks, push/local notifications

Children

Kinetly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

You can use Kinetly without ever granting camera or Screen Time permission; the related features simply won't work. You can request deletion of any analytics data associated with your device by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change too.
