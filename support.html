Common questions

The camera doesn't recognize my stretch.
Make sure your full upper body (or the side of your body, for stretches marked "side-on") is visible in frame, and that the room is reasonably lit. Pose checks compare against your own posture, not a fixed standard, so it may take a moment to register.

My apps didn't unlock after I stretched.
Make sure you held the pose until the on-screen countdown finished for every stretch in the routine — the block only lifts once the last one is done.

The app block isn't showing up at my scheduled time.
Check that Screen Time permission is granted (Settings → Screen Time inside Kinetly, or iOS Settings → Screen Time), and that the reminder is switched on with at least one app selected in the Schedule tab.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID: go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kinetly. You can also reach this from Kinetly's Settings tab under "Manage Subscription."

Does Kinetly see my camera feed or my screen time data?
No. Pose detection runs entirely on your phone, and nothing is recorded or transmitted. See our Privacy Policy for details.
